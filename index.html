50+ Years of Quality Craftsmanship in Monroe

For over 50 years, Cusumano Home Improvements has been the trusted choice for homeowners throughout Monroe, Michigan and surrounding areas. As a family-owned and operated business, we bring three generations of craftsmanship and expertise to every project.

Our comprehensive home improvement services include custom deck construction, professional roofing installation and repair, quality siding solutions, complete bathroom and kitchen remodeling, garage construction, and convenient dumpster rental services. Whether you're planning a small repair or a complete home transformation, our experienced team has the skills and dedication to exceed your expectations.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality, attention to detail, and personalized service. We understand that your home is your most important investment, and we treat every project with the care and precision it deserves. From the initial consultation to final cleanup, we work closely with you to ensure your vision becomes reality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve in Michigan?

We primarily serve Monroe, Michigan and surrounding areas including Temperance, Carleton, Flat Rock, and nearby communities. For projects outside our typical service area, please contact us to discuss options.

What factors affect home improvement project pricing?

Project costs vary based on several factors including size, materials chosen, design complexity, site conditions, and specific features requested. Every project is unique, which is why we provide free, detailed estimates tailored to your specific needs and preferences.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation estimates for all our home improvement services. Contact us to schedule your consultation and discuss your project needs.

How long has Cusumano Home Improvements been in business?

We have over 50 years of experience in the home improvement industry, providing quality craftsmanship to Monroe and surrounding areas since 1973.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, we maintain all necessary licensing and insurance coverage as required by law for home improvement contractors in Michigan. This provides protection and peace of mind for our clients.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including cash, check, and major credit cards for your convenience. Payment terms and schedules are discussed during project planning to ensure a smooth process.

How long does a typical home improvement project take?

Project timelines vary significantly based on scope, complexity, weather conditions, and other factors. During your consultation, we'll discuss realistic timelines and keep you informed throughout the process to ensure your project stays on track.

What quality assurance do you provide?

We stand behind our workmanship and use quality materials from reputable suppliers. We'll discuss project-specific quality assurance measures during your consultation to ensure you understand what to expect.
